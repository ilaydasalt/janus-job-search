Privacy Policy

Janus Job Search is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to other users.

The app accesses the owner's Google Sheets (to store job postings) and Gmail (to send the owner a summary email). Data stays in the owner's own Google account and on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
